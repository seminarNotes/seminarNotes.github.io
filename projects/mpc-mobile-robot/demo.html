Video Demonstrations

Simulation and experimental results of Neural ODE-based MPC for mobile robot trajectory tracking

Case 1: Circular Trajectory

The robot tracks a circular reference path (r=1m) at constant speed. The MPC controller smoothly follows the curve with minimal tracking error. Blue trajectory = reference, Red trajectory = robot path.

Case 2: Ellipse Trajectory

The robot follows an elliptical path (a=1.5m, b=0.8m). The Neural ODE-based MPC captures the error dynamics and provides predictive control, achieving excellent tracking performance.

Case 3: Lemniscate (Self-Intersecting Path)

This challenging self-intersecting trajectory demonstrates the robustness of the approach. The scheduling variable $\rho = [\mathbf{q}_d, \dot{\mathbf{q}}_d]$ enables the MPC to handle non-convex path geometries without confusion at the intersection point.

PD vs MPC Comparison

Side-by-side comparison of PD baseline control (left) and Neural ODE-based MPC (right). The MPC controller exhibits superior tracking accuracy, smoother control inputs, and faster error convergence compared to the fixed-gain PD controller.
