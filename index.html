😎 About me

I build learning-based controllers that come with mathematical proof, not just empirical success. My work bridges Neural ODE dynamics learning, Model Predictive Control, and formal stability theory, validated on real robots and industrial hardware.



🤖 Experiments

I integrate Neural ODE models into Nonlinear MPC and validate tracking and imitation learning performance through simulation and rigorous real-world hardware experiments.

🧪 Simulation Studies

I address model uncertainty in Euler-Lagrange dynamics by learning error dynamics via a Linear Parameter-Varying (LPV) Neural ODE, from which an imitation learning-based control policy is derived.
