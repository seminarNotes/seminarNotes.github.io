😎 About me

I am a control engineer and researcher specializing in data-driven optimal control for high-performance robotic systems. My research stands at the intersection of machine learning and control theory, focusing on developing frameworks that combine Neural ODE-based error dynamics learning with Nonlinear Model Predictive Control (NMPC).

Background: I hold a unique dual-disciplinary background, having earned an M.S. in Mathematics (Focus: Financial Mathematics) followed by an M.S. in Engineering (Focus: Data-Driven Control). Before transitioning full-time into robotics, I worked as a Financial Engineer/Data Analyst at KIS Pricing, where I developed derivative valuation modules and managed robust data pipelines.

Research & Approach: My current research addresses a critical challenge in learning-based control: overcoming performance degradation caused by dynamics model mismatch while ensuring theoretical rigor. I leverage control-theoretic tools such as Lyapunov stability theory, Contraction theory, and Input-to-State Stability (ISS) to achieve safe, data-efficient, and certified neural-network-embedded control systems.

Validation: I bridge theoretical mathematical proofs with practical engineering validation. My approaches have been successfully verified through simulation and hardware experiments, including autonomous mobile robots, magnetic levitation systems, and multi-DOF robot manipulators.

Main Research Contribution & Outputs: I have a solid track record of translating theoretical insights into peer-reviewed publications across both mathematics and control engineering. Currently, I have two journal papers under peer review at top-tier venues: IEEE Transactions on Cybernetics and the International Journal of Robust and Nonlinear Control (IJRNC). Additionally, my foundational research in mathematical finance was published in the Bulletin of the Korean Mathematical Society (SCIE) in 2023. My conference outcomes include a published paper in the Proceedings of ICCAS 2025 and a recently accepted paper for IFAC 2026.



🤖 Experiments

I integrate Neural ODE models into Nonlinear MPC and validate tracking and imitation learning performance through simulation and rigorous real-world hardware experiments.
